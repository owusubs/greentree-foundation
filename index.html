How help moves

Simple, transparent, and close to the people we serve.

  1. Listen locally Community workers identify urgent needs with families, teachers, and clinics.
  2. Match resources Donations and volunteer time are assigned to the program where they can help fastest.
  3. Report impact Partners share outcomes so supporters can see what changed and where help is still needed.
