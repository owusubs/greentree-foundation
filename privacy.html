What we collect

Information from forms and donations.

When you contact us, volunteer, subscribe to updates, or donate, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, availability, message, donation amount, and Stripe payment reference.

Payment card details are handled by Stripe. GreenTree Foundation does not receive or store full card numbers through this website.

How we use it

Only for the purpose you selected.

Volunteer and contact details are used to reply to your enquiry and organise suitable next steps. Newsletter details are used to send community updates where you have asked to receive them.

Donation records are used to process payments, prevent fraud, maintain financial records, and understand supporter activity.

Your choices

Access, correction, and unsubscribe.

You can ask GreenTree Foundation to correct, delete, or provide a copy of your personal information where the law allows. Newsletter messages should include an unsubscribe route before the site is used publicly.
